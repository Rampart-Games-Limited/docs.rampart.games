# Communities

Adding a "Community" to your account allows your gaming group to contribute to the cost of running your servers

The community is essentially a community wallet, separate to your personal wallet, that can be used to pay for subscriptions

You can add members (admins) to your community to help manage it, though anybody can contribute funds to the community wallet without being a registered member

Think carefully about who you assign permissions to, as any community credits spent by members are non-refundable
